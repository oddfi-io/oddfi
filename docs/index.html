How it works

From wallet connect to payout, every step that touches money happens on-chain.

Connect

Sign in with MetaMask or WalletConnect. No email, no password.

Stake

Stake ODD to unlock VIP tiers and higher daily limits.

Bet

1X2, Asian Handicap, Over/Under and parlays, with live odds pushed over WebSocket.

Settle

Results come from two data sources. Settlement goes on-chain through a Gnosis Safe multisig.

Claim

Winners claim from the contract. Fees fund stakers, a reserve buffer and referrers.

Architecture

Five layers. The Go services share state only through MySQL and Redis.

The contracts hold the funds. Off-chain services handle odds, risk checks and indexing, and they call the contracts through a typed Go SDK with separate admin, relay and read clients.

Smart contracts

Five upgradeable contracts on BSC, built and tested with Foundry.

ODDToken

Clean BEP-20

100M fixed supply. No inflation, no burn, no transfer tax. Pausable, UUPS-upgradeable.

StakingPool

Stake for status

Effective stake sets one of 10 VIP tiers in real time and receives 2% of every payout.

LiquidityPool

House liquidity

A global pool plus per-match pools, with LP positions, requestable liquidity and payout windows.

BettingPool

Markets & bets

Opens markets, places bets, runs risk checks, handles refunds, claims, referral rewards and a three-tier reserve waterfall.

BettingSettlementModule

Settlement via delegatecall

Holds the heavy settlement and refund logic so BettingPool stays under the bytecode size limit.

Gnosis Safe

Multisig settlement

No single key can settle a market. The SDK can propose, execute, or export raw calldata.
